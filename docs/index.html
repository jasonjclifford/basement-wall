Current plan — status summary

A 3/4" plywood ladder frame (bottom plate, 3 stiles, top plate) carries two infill KALLAX bookcases, a paint-grade plywood backer + board-and-batten facing on each room's side, and a plywood upper panel closing the gap up to the ceiling. A structural review (Sept 2026) found and resolved several framing/panel gaps — see build reference's "Structural review findings" — and flagged a handful of items still open below.

  • Structure: the ladder frame — the centre stile, the staggered bottom/top plate splices, and the merged outer stile / wall cleat at each side — is detailed in ladder framing.
  • Fit: each KALLAX bay is cut 1/2" wider than its unit so the boxes float rather than needing a press fit, with the battens covering the reveal. This is the design's most important idea: it converts an accumulating tolerance problem into a forgiving one. See the build reference's "Read this first — how this design thinks".
  • Facing: each backer (1/4" paint-grade plywood) gets board-and-batten trim — a 1x4 batten centred on each of the 3 stiles (shared between the backers on either side of it, and wide enough to cover the play in the KALLAX bays), plus one 1x3 batten down the middle of each backer face (covers the 4x4's two-board seam; decorative on the single-board 4x3), capped by a horizontal 1x3 cap rail running the full width at the top of the KALLAX — that rail covers the top plate's bare plywood edge and closes the light gap left by a bookcase shorter than the frame's design height, so it is structural to the light-blocking goal rather than decorative. Bedroom side (4x4 backer, KALLAX): painted very light pink, white KALLAX. Office side (4x3 backer, KALLAX): color still open.
  • Bedroom desk (44"x23" antique wood desk) sits flush against the divider's 4x4 backer, right end against the internal wall — covers the right ~44" of that backer, ~14 1/2" of pink backer stays exposed to its left. See bedroom elevation.
  • Office furniture run (tall Alex + short Alex + 79" desktop + short Alex, 93 1/8" total) sits flush against the divider, tall Alex's outer side against the external wall, trailing toward the internal wall with a ~12" gap short of it. Covers the entire 4x3 backer plus most of the 4x4's open shelving — about 10 15/16" of the 4x4's internal-wall end stays open. See office elevation.
  • Both furniture placements together, top-down, in the plan view.
  • Upper panel: 1/2" paint-grade plywood (switched from MDF for basement moisture tolerance; kept at 1/2" rather than the backers' 1/4" because it is a free membrane with nothing behind it, unlike the carcass-backed backers), 2 pieces — a single seam at the ceiling step, so both pieces are plain rectangles rather than L-shapes, covered by a 1x2 pine cap strip. Centred in the divider's depth, which leaves a ~7 3/4" ledge open to each room. Simplified from 3 pieces plus seam blocking — see the build reference's "Why the upper panel got simpler". Detailed in the side-view section.
  • Tipping resistance: two steel angle brackets tie the center stile to the ceiling (joist if one lands near center, drywall anchors otherwise), and a plywood scab reinforces the top-plate splice — added after a structural review found the frame's center span had no connection to the building. See build reference.
  • Track light junction box: approach decided — the panel's opening is placed just past the box on the bedroom side rather than cutting into the panel, leaving the box accessible without a cutout/curb/trim — but a structural review flagged the feasibility math as still unverified pending the field measurement below. See build reference for the full writeup.

Open:

  • Track light — field measurement of the box/track's position and projection past the wall plane, needed both to place it precisely in the drawings and to confirm the panel-edge approach actually clears the box.
  • Office backer paint color (currently drawn neutral/undecided).
  • Ceiling joist direction/locations and wall stud locations on both walls — needed to place the ceiling brackets and confirm the wall cleats land on studs.
  • Inside-casing width at mid-height and near the ceiling (only the floor-level reading exists so far) — confirms the frame clears the opening at every height, not just at the floor.
  • A full fastener schedule for the remaining unspecified joints (backer-to-carcass, baseboard-to-plate).
