The outer stile and the wall cleat are one member, not two stacked pieces — it screws to the studs, takes the backer edge, and passes in front of the baseboard so nothing needs notching. Rip it to whatever thickness clears your baseboard.

Width is tight here: the opening is ~105 1/4" and the two bookcases are 101 13/16" of it, leaving ~3 7/16" for all three vertical members plus the fitting slack. That's why this is one piece rather than two — see the build reference if you're considering changing it.
