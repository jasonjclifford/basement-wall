Wall support detail — side gap & cleat (either end, both are 3/4")

Looking down (plan) — wall to stile

Looking at the wall (elevation) — baseboard to ceiling

The whole assembly sits 3/4" clear of each side wall so it passes in front of the baseboard without notching. Above the baseboard, that 3/4" gap takes a cleat screwed into the studs — this is both the light seal and the lag point for the stiles. Below the cleat, the baseboard fills the gap. The stile itself is screwed to the cleat through its face (driven from the room side before the backer panel goes on) — not through the cleat from inside the gap.
