Current plan — status summary

A 3/4" plywood ladder frame (bottom plate, 3 stiles, top plate) carries two infill KALLAX bookcases, a paint-grade plywood backer + board-and-batten facing on each room's side, and a plywood upper panel closing the gap up to the ceiling. Design is complete except the two open items at the bottom.

  • Structure: the ladder frame — stiles, bottom/top plate splices, and the wall-cleat support at each side gap — is detailed in ladder framing.
  • Facing: each backer (1/4" paint-grade plywood) gets board-and-batten trim — a 1x3 batten centered on each of the 3 stiles (shared between the backers on either side of it), plus one 1x3 batten down the middle of each backer face (covers the 4x4's two-board seam; decorative on the single-board 4x3). Bedroom side (4x4 backer, KALLAX): painted very light pink, white KALLAX. Office side (4x3 backer, KALLAX): color still open.
  • Bedroom desk (44"x23" antique wood desk) sits flush against the divider's 4x4 backer, right end against the internal wall — covers the right ~44" of that backer, ~13 13/16" of pink backer stays exposed to its left. See bedroom elevation.
  • Office furniture run (tall Alex + short Alex + 79" desktop + short Alex, 93 1/8" total) sits flush against the divider, tall Alex's outer side against the external wall, trailing toward the internal wall with a ~12" gap short of it. Covers the entire 4x3 backer plus most of the 4x4's open shelving — about 10 15/16" of the 4x4's internal-wall end stays open. See office elevation.
  • Both furniture placements together, top-down, in the plan view.
  • Upper panel: 1/2" paint-grade plywood (switched from MDF for basement moisture tolerance; kept at 1/2" rather than the backers' 1/4" since it spans unsupported between cleats), 3 pieces with the 2 seams covered by 1x2 pine cap strips, centered in the divider's depth. Detailed in the side-view section.
  • Track light junction box: decided — the panel's opening is placed just past the box on the bedroom side rather than cutting into the panel, leaving the box accessible without a cutout/curb/trim. See build reference for the full writeup.

Open:

  • Office backer paint color (currently drawn neutral/undecided).
